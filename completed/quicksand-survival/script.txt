Quicksand can't swallow you, but it can still kill you.
Movies lied: you only sink to about your waist.
Quicksand is twice as dense as your body, so you float.
The real trap is getting your legs back out.
Yanking one foot free takes the force of lifting a car.
So if friends pull hard, they could tear you apart.
Instead, slowly wiggle your legs in little circles.
That lets water seep in and loosens the sand's grip.
Lean back, spread out, and float your legs free.
Don't dawdle: near the sea, the rising tide is the real killer.
