auto-brewery-gut: scene direction (v3 toolkit: kit.py + actor.py + shot.py; one Actor, three sets; see scene.py)

Cast: him = blend/char_hero.blend. No second character (the officer and the doctors stay off-screen).
Sets: ROAD (night traffic stop: his navy sedan parked at the kerb, a white police car behind it with a strobing
   red/blue light bar and headlights on him, street lamps, trees, two traffic cones), HOSP (hospital room: bed,
   IV pole with drip chamber, vitals monitor, window, nightstand; he sits on the bed edge) and XRAY (a cutaway of
   his small intestine: glowing wall, villi, budding yeast cells, crumbs, golden ethanol bubbles, a vessel below).
   Set changes are hard cuts with a whoosh and an exposure spike. No on-screen text; the breathalyzer reading is a
   7-segment prop display made of geometry.
Frame 1 (thumbnail): medium close-up of his startled face, lit red and blue by the light bar behind him.

Beat 1 HOOK "Arrested for drunk driving..."  LightsFace (50mm push-in, strobe on his face) -> RoadWide (28mm, him,
   his car, the police car, lamps).
Beat 2 "A breathalyzer read 0.41..."  BlowMed (45mm: he blows, cheeks puffed) -> MeterECU (85mm insert on the
   device; the display counts up to 0.41 and flashes red; camera kick on the reading).
Beat 3 "He'd been arrested twice..."  RoofLow (28mm low angle, palms slap onto the car roof, lights behind) ->
   SoberFace (60mm: he turns to camera and shakes his head).
Beat 4 "The culprit was living inside his gut."  BodyX (his body turns X-ray blue, his belly glows amber with
   bubbles; the camera plunges into the belly) -> GutDive (arrive inside the gut, pull back to reveal it).
Beat 5 "Yeast down there was fermenting his food into alcohol."  YeastOrbit (40mm orbit, bubbles rising off the
   cells) -> YeastMacro (85mm on one budding cell).
Beat 6 "Doctors proved it in hospital by feeding him sugar..."  HospWide (28mm, him on the bed, IV, monitor) ->
   DrinkCU (55mm: he gulps an orange glucose drink, two gulps).
Beat 7 MONEY MOMENT "His blood alcohol shot past 0.4 percent..."  SugarSlomo (timewarp ~0.4x: sugar crystals flood
   in, hit the yeast, the cells swell and flare, a burst of golden bubbles, the vessel turns amber; camera kick) ->
   DizzyCU (55mm orbit: drunk sway, heavy eyelids, a loose smile).
Beat 8 "Antifungal drugs finally shut the brewery down."  IVDrip (85mm on the drip chamber) -> YeastDie (blue
   drug particles reach the cells, they grey and shrink, the bubbles stop).
Beat 9 "It's called auto-brewery syndrome..."  HospOrbit (35mm orbit, him recovered on the bed).
Beat 10 "Your gut can be a brewery, so believe the sober patient."  BreweryX (X-ray body, belly glowing) -> Closer
   (55mm push-in to his relieved smile).
19 shots, every one moving; one slow-motion moment (beat 7).
