Cast: her = blend/char_heroine.blend (never replaced by the male hero). No other character.
Sets: HOME (sunny living room, five photo frames on the wall, window right), LAB (anatomy lab: steel table under a sheet, lamp,
   bench with skull, instrument tray; she is not shown), ORGAN (her torso organs parented to her spine on a black void:
   ribs, spine, translucent lungs, heart, liver, stomach, spleen, bowel, aorta, vena cava, flowing beads), GRID (22,000 glowing
   dots on a mirror floor and one amber dot). No on-screen text. Set changes are hard cuts with a whoosh + exposure spike.
