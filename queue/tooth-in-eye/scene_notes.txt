SCENE NOTES: tooth-in-eye  (~42 s total, 10 beats)

ANIMATION LIBRARY NOTE
The repo has no animation/ folder, so `ls animation/` failed. The only retargeted Mixamo clips the
pipeline references are in D:\ShortsPipeline\blend\anims\ (see scripts/apply_retargeted_action.py):
  - Walking.blend   (action "Walking")
  - FallingIdle.blend (action "FallingIdle")
Every beat below uses one of these two, or the base rig held in a static pose ("HOLD: rest pose",
posed by hand on the mixamorig bones). If more Mixamo FBX files get downloaded (e.g. Sitting Idle, Looking
Around, Rubbing Eyes, Lying Down), swap them in where marked "PREFERRED IF AVAILABLE".

BEAT 1: "Doctors put this man's tooth inside his eye, to cure his blindness."  (~4.5 s)
- Camera: extreme close-up on one eye, then a fast pull-back to a mid-shot of his face.
- Character animation: HOLD rest pose with slight head turn (keyframed on mixamorig:Head).
- Key visual: x-ray overlay over the eye showing a tooth silhouette where the cornea should be. Text pop "TOOTH 🦷 → EYE 👁️".
- Lighting mood: dramatic, cold rim light, dark background.

BEAT 2: "At thirteen, a reaction to ibuprofen blinded him."  (~3.5 s)
- Camera: mid-shot, slight dolly in. Younger version of the character (scale 0.85).
- Character animation: FallingIdle (trim to the first ~1 s for a stumble/sway), then HOLD.
- Key visual: a pill drops in, then a red rash spreads over the skin and both corneas cloud milky white. Text pop "AGE 13".
- Lighting mood: warm, then a sharp shift to cold blue as the eyes cloud.

BEAT 3: "Cornea transplants failed for twenty years."  (~3 s)
- Camera: wide shot, time-lapse.
- Character animation: Walking (slow, in place on a treadmill loop) while the character ages up.
- Key visual: counter ticking "1… 12 transplants" plus a calendar flipping to "20 YEARS". The cornea graft turns cloudy again each time.
- Lighting mood: cold, desaturated.

BEAT 4: "So surgeons pulled his canine tooth, with a sliver of jawbone."  (~4 s)
- Camera: POV from inside the mouth looking at the upper canine; cut to a cross-section of the jaw.
- Character animation: HOLD lying pose (rotate rig 90° onto a surgical table; PREFERRED IF AVAILABLE: Mixamo "Lying Down").
- Key visual: cross-section highlight in yellow on the canine plus a thin bone layer, which lifts out. Text pop "CANINE TOOTH".
- Lighting mood: clinical white surgical light from above.

BEAT 5: "They shaved it into a tiny block and drilled in a plastic lens."  (~4.5 s)
- Camera: macro close-up on the tooth on a tray, rotating turntable.
- Character animation: none (prop-only shot).
- Key visual: tooth shaved down into a rectangular block, a drill bores through, then a clear acrylic cylinder slides in with a glint. Text pop "4 mm".
- Lighting mood: clinical, crisp specular highlights.

BEAT 6: "Then they sewed it into his cheek for months to grow blood vessels."  (~4.5 s)
- Camera: close-up of the face at 3/4 angle with an x-ray overlay on the cheek.
- Character animation: HOLD rest pose, small idle breathing (scale the spine bone by about 1%).
- Key visual: the tooth-lens block glows under the cheek skin while red vessels branch into it. Calendar text pop "~3 MONTHS".
- Lighting mood: warm, soft.

BEAT 7: "Finally, it went into his eye."  (~2.5 s)
- Camera: snap zoom from cheek to eye.
- Character animation: HOLD lying pose (surgery table).
- Key visual: the block slides out of the cheek, the cloudy cornea surface peels away, the block seats into the eye, then quick stitches.
- Lighting mood: dramatic, high contrast.

BEAT 8: "His body is less likely to reject his own tooth."  (~3.5 s)
- Camera: internal "body view" wide shot.
- Character animation: none (internal cutaway).
- Key visual: immune cells (green blobs) approach the tooth, sniff it, and turn away. Text pop "SELF ✔".
- Lighting mood: warm, glowing bioluminescent.

BEAT 9: "And it worked. He made eye contact with his surgeon."  (~4.5 s)
- Camera: POV through the lens: a blurry then sharp image of the surgeon's face. Cut to an over-the-shoulder two-shot.
- Character animation: HOLD rest pose with a head lift toward the surgeon. Surgeon character uses Walking (2-3 steps in, then stop).
- Key visual: a vignette circle that matches the small lens field of view. The world snaps into focus.
- Lighting mood: warm golden, emotional.

BEAT 10: "Turns out, your teeth are a backup plan for your eyes."  (~4 s)
- Camera: close-up face to camera, then pull back to a mid-shot. The character wears sunglasses (he has glare issues).
- Character animation: Walking (out of the hospital toward camera).
- Key visual: the character smiles, then glints a canine; text pop "🦷 = 👁️ BACKUP".
- Lighting mood: bright daylight, warm.
