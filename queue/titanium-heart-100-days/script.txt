This man lived over a hundred days with a titanium heart.
His own heart was failing, with no donor in sight.
So in 2024, surgeons in Sydney replaced it completely.
The new heart has one moving part, a spinning rotor.
Magnets keep it floating, so nothing rubs or wears out.
It pumps blood to his body and lungs.
In February, he walked out of the hospital with it.
Then a donor heart arrived, and he got his transplant.
Its inventor made it after his dad died of heart failure.
Someday, hearts like this could end the wait for donors.
