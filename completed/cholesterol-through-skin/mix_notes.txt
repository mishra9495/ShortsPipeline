cholesterol-through-skin — sound design direction (numpy + soundfile, matches mix_template.py style)

Voice: queue narration.wav / Kokoro am_fenrir per daily_render.ps1's fallback chain. One narrator.
Music: quiet, slightly ominous synth/drone bed under beats 1-6 (curiosity + unease, not horror),
  warming into a brighter major pad from beat 9 (the closer) — mirrors brain-leaking-nose's
  dark-to-resolve arc but inverted irony: the "resolve" chord lands UNDER a line that is actually
  a warning ("arteries were quietly in danger"), so keep it warm/pleasant, not triumphant.
  -18 dB under the voice, -12 dB in gaps. Dip to near-silence ~0.3-0.5s before the vial reveal
  (beat 6) and again right before the eruption (beat 9), per the "hard dip before reveal" rule.

Per-beat SFX (cue every effect from beat times + fractions, same at(i, frac) pattern as scene.py;
keep the fraction constants literally in sync with scene.py's SYNC block):
1. Hook (palm ECU): a soft, wet "squelch" under the push-in (something unpleasant, already there);
   low sub-hum under the whole hook.
2. Kitchen wide: ambient room tone, a soft knife/scrape as he cuts cheese, a chair creak.
3. Food insert: a louder cheese-cut "thwack", a soft butter-knife scrape, meat squelch on the steak.
4. Face medium (feels great): a contented exhale/hum (non-verbal), a soft chew/swallow foley.
5. Palm shock: a sharp little "huh" breath-catch cue (foley, not VO), a soft low sting (rising tone)
   starting ~0.1s before the line ends, same `sting()` synth style as brain-leaking-nose's mix.py.
6. Vial insert: glass clink as the vial lifts, a soft pour/gurgle for the separating layers, a low
   uneasy drone swell.
7. Plunge into vessel (reveal begins): whoosh in (rising), then a steady, slightly sped-up heartbeat
   under the vessel orbit (bpm ~95, tenser than resting).
8. Plaque orbit: continuing heartbeat (bpm creeping to ~105), a low crackly "building pressure" rumble.
9. MONEY MOMENT (eruption, slow-mo): whoosh build into a wet pop/burst transient at the exact erupt
   frame, time-stretched slightly to match the slow-mo visual, then a sub-boom; heartbeat cuts out
   for the slomo window itself (silence sells slow motion) and resumes right after.
10. Closer: heartbeat settles back to resting (bpm 70), a warm major chime/pluck resolve under
   "he felt incredible", then a single low uneasy drone note lands under "quietly in danger" —
   the music should NOT resolve fully; leave a small dissonant tail into the +36 frame hold.

Master: -14 LUFS integrated, -1 dBTP (ffmpeg loudnorm at mux, as in every other queue item).
