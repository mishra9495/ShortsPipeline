cholesterol-through-skin — scene direction (v3 toolkit: kit.py + actor.py + shot.py, one Actor, one set)

Cast: him = blend/char_hero.blend (opened as the main file). No second character needed.
Set: ONE location, "Home" — a small dining nook (kit.room + an inline dining table/chair), built once.
   An "Anatomy" prop group (vessel + plaque + crystal) sits coincident with his right palm in world
   space (same point, macro scale), hidden by hide_render except during beats 6-8. The camera's
   plunge_in/plunge_out moves use that shared world point so the "fly into the skin" cut reads as
   continuous even though it is a hard cut between two Blender collections.
Frame 1 (thumbnail): his right palm, already resting up on the table, 3-4 small yellow nodules
   clearly lit and in focus — the whole premise visible before a word is spoken.

Beat 1 (0.0-B1) — HOOK. "This man ate so much cheese, cholesterol started leaking through his skin."
  Camera: PalmHookECU, 85mm macro, slow push-in on his right palm (already in motion at frame 1).
  Character: right hand resting palm-up on the table edge, fingers relaxed and open (not curled) so
    the nodules read clearly; idle breathing/sway only.
  Visual: 3-4 pale-yellow waxy nodule clusters (xanthoma) on the palm, subtle sheen.
  Shots: 1 (PalmHookECU, ~2.2s), then a quick 10-15deg orbit tightening the push (PalmHookOrbit, ~1.3s).

Beat 2 (B1-B2) — "He was eight months into an all-meat carnivore diet."
  Camera: KitchenWide, 35mm, slow push/orbit, establishes him seated at the dining table.
  Character: seated, relaxed posture, left hand reaches for the cheese wheel.
  Props in frame: cheese wheel, butter stick on a dish, a plate with a raw-looking steak, fork+knife.
  Shots: 1 (KitchenWide, ~2.6s).

Beat 3 — "Up to nine pounds of cheese and butter, every day."
  Camera: FoodInsertCU, 60mm, push-in insert on the food pile (no character in frame).
  Visual: the cheese wedge, butter stick with a bite/scoop missing, steak — "hero props" detail.
  Shots: 1 (FoodInsertCU, ~2.6s).

Beat 4 — "He said he'd never felt better: more energy, less weight."
  Camera: FaceMedium, 50mm, slow orbit, face on the upper-third line.
  Character: left hand brings a piece of food to his mouth (bite cycle), content restrained smile
    (mouthSmileLeft/Right, cheekSquint), slight head tilt up ("feeling good").
  Shots: 1 (FaceMedium, ~2.9s).

Beat 5 — "Then yellow lumps appeared on his palms, soles, and elbows."
  Camera: PalmShockECU, 85mm, push-in, returns to the SAME right palm as frame 1.
  Character: head turns and tilts down toward his own hand, brows up (browInnerUp), small jawOpen —
    a beat of surprise at something he is noticing for the first time (he "touches" it with his
    gaze, not his hand, so the palm framing stays clean). Eating motion pauses.
  Shots: 1 (PalmShockECU, ~2.6s).

Beat 6 — "Doctors ran his blood work and were stunned."
  Camera: VialInsert, 85mm, neutral dark tabletop (no new room — Zack D "object explainer" style),
    push-in as a gloved hand (forearm only, in frame edge) lifts a blood-draw vial into the light.
  Visual: the vial's blood has visibly separated — a thick, pale creamy-yellow layer floating on
    top of the dark red serum (lipemic blood: the real, true visual sign of extreme hypercholesterolemia;
    no on-screen numbers/text, the visual itself carries the shock).
  Shots: 1 (VialInsert, ~2.6s).

Beat 7 — "His cholesterol had shot past one thousand milligrams per deciliter." REVEAL BEGINS.
  Camera: PlungeIn, 100mm macro orbit, hard cut from the vial insert (whoosh SFX + a brief exposure
    spike sell the "zoom into the bloodstream" jump — no literal camera plunge_in/out between the two
    sets, since they are not spatially coincident). Arrives on a glowing, translucent arteriole
    (edge_glow wall material) with bright red flowing "blood" inside.
  Visual: yellow plaque blobs along the inner vessel wall grow in number/size continuously from this
    beat into the next (bake scale 0->1, staggered starts) — the core "process" transformation.
  Shots: 1 (PlungeIn/VesselMacro, ~2.6s).

Beat 8 — "That's nearly five times what doctors call healthy."
  Camera: PlaqueOrbit, 100mm, orbit continues around the vessel; plaque is now thick, narrowing the
    glowing red "blood flow" core to a thin strand against the yellow-crusted wall.
  Shots: 1 (PlaqueOrbit, ~2.6s).

Beat 9 — "Those yellow bumps were pure fat, pushed straight out of his blood." MONEY MOMENT (slow-mo).
  Camera: EruptBuildCU, 100mm, fast push onto the single largest plaque mass as it swells (~0.9s),
    then EruptSlomo, same lens, holds as that mass breaks through the translucent vessel wall and
    bulges outward (shape-key / scale pop with a bright flash + whoosh), timewarped to about 0.35x
    for ~1.1s of screen time — the one slow-motion beat in the short. Cut (hard cut, not continuous)
    back to Home immediately after.
  Shots: 2 (EruptBuildCU ~0.9s, EruptSlomo ~2.0s).

Beat 10 — "He felt incredible. His arteries were quietly in danger." CLOSER (loops to frame 1).
  Camera: CloserPull, 85mm, hard cut back from the eruption to the SAME right palm (echoing frame
    1's framing almost exactly), then a slow pull-back revealing him in the soft background, still
    eating, relaxed and smiling, nodules now reading plainly on his palm.
  Shots: 1 (CloserPull, ~3.0s) + hold through the +36 frame tail.

Total: 12 shots, 1.9-3.0 s each, mean ~2.6 s — inside the 12-20 shot / 1.5-3 s Zack D target.
Lighting: warm daylight interior (key ~320W warm area light, soft fill) for Home; the Anatomy void
  uses near-black world strength ~0.03, a blood-red ambient tint, strong rim + emissive glow on the
  vessel/plaque, matching STYLE_GUIDE section 5.
No on-screen text anywhere. No captions. Daniel/Kokoro narration only.
