She lived ninety-nine years with her organs on the wrong side.
Rose Bentley raised five kids, loved swimming, and suspected nothing.
She died in 2017 and donated her body to science.
Medical students in Oregon began studying her body.
They examined her heart, and the blood vessels looked wrong.
Her liver and stomach had swapped sides, like a mirror image.
Only her heart sat normally, so the secret stayed hidden.
It strikes about one in twenty-two thousand births.
Usually it brings heart defects, yet she's the oldest known case.
Her last gift taught future doctors to expect the unexpected.
