titanium-heart-100-days: scene direction (v3 toolkit: kit.py + actor.py + shot.py; one Actor, six sets; see scene.py)

Cast: him = blend/char_hero.blend. No second character (surgeons and the inventor are implied by props only).
Animation: procedural on the mixamorig bones with scripts/actor.py (CLAUDE.md: the Mixamo retarget is unvalidated).
   Closest Mixamo clips in animation_manifest.txt, for reference only, NOT used: "Injured Hurting Idle.fbx" (beat 2),
   "Laying Mild Cough.fbx" (beats 3 and 8, lying on the table), "Waving.fbx" (beat 7).
Sets: WARD (cardiac ward corner: bed, vital monitor, IV pole, privacy curtain, window), OR (table, lamp, monitor, tray, a donor-heart
   cooler on a steel trolley), EXIT (hospital lobby, automatic glass doors onto a sunny forecourt with trees), WORKSHOP (the inventor's
   bench: brass pump prototypes, copper pipe, pegboard tools, a framed photo, a warm lamp, a glass prototype heart),
   DEVICE (the titanium heart alone in a deep-blue void, glass front so the floating rotor shows),
   XRAY (his body as a glowing blue shell; ribs, spine, lungs, aorta, pulmonary arteries, and whichever heart is in him).
   Set changes are hard cuts with a whoosh and an exposure spike. No on-screen text.
The titanium heart: a 70 mm drum with two inflow cuffs at the back, two grafts on top (aorta, pulmonary artery), one rotor with swept vanes
   on both faces, two cyan magnet rings above and below its rim and a glowing levitation gap. Blood cells spiral in and out of it.
Frame 1 (thumbnail): the titanium heart glowing in his X-ray chest, rotor spinning, camera pushing in.

Beat 1 HOOK "This man lived over a hundred days with a titanium heart."  HookX (50mm push-in on the X-ray chest, cool, dramatic)
   -> HookFace (ward, 50mm, he looks down at his chest, hand flat over his heart, eyes wide). ~1.6 s each.
Beat 2 "His own heart was failing, with no donor in sight."  WardWide (32mm slow orbit: slumped, short of breath, hand on chest, monitor;
   cold daylight) -> SickX (60mm push on the dull, weak, irregularly beating heart; flow dots crawl).
Beat 3 "So in 2024, surgeons in Sydney replaced it completely."  ORWide (30mm high angle, he lies sedated, plunge toward his chest)
   -> SwapX (X-ray: the failing heart lifts out toward camera, the titanium heart slides in; boom, camera kick, exposure flash).
Beat 4 "The new heart has one moving part, a spinning rotor."  DeviceOrbit (85mm orbit round the device, key + cyan rim)
   -> RotorMacro (100mm push-in through the glass on the spinning rotor).
Beat 5 MONEY MOMENT "Magnets keep it floating, so nothing rubs or wears out."  FloatSlomo (slow motion ~0.4x on the rotor and blood:
   100mm three-quarter orbit, the magnet rings flare cyan, the gap glows; music dips to silence before it). ~3 s.
Beat 6 "It pumps blood to his body and lungs."  FlowX (X-ray, 40mm: red dots stream up the aorta, plum dots out to both lungs)
   -> FlowMacro (85mm from above: red and plum cells spiral round the rotor and out of the two grafts).
Beat 7 "In February, he walked out of the hospital with it."  ExitWide (28mm from behind: the glass doors glide apart, sunlight floods in,
   controller bag at his hip) -> ExitFace (50mm reverse from the doorway: warm sun on his face, deep breath, smile).
Beat 8 "Then a donor heart arrived, and he got his transplant."  CoolerCU (40mm: the cooler trolley rolls in and settles; he lies in the
   OR behind) -> DonorX (X-ray, 55mm: the titanium heart shrinks away and a rosy, strongly beating donor heart takes its place).
Beat 9 "Its inventor made it after his dad died of heart failure."  WorkshopWide (35mm, warm lamp, focus on the framed photo)
   -> ProtoMacro (85mm orbit round the glass prototype, rotor turning).
Beat 10 "Someday, hearts like this could end the wait for donors."  DeviceHero (85mm reverse orbit, hero light)
   -> CloserX (X-ray push-in that matches frame 1, so the replay loops).
19 shots, every one moving, 1.5-3 s each at the real narration pace; one slow-motion moment (beat 5).
Lighting: cool clinical (ward, OR), dark blue void with cyan rims (X-ray, device), warm sunlight (exit), warm tungsten (workshop).
Artistic license: the X-ray device is drawn ~40% larger than life and its housing glows so it reads; the macro housing is clear.
Tested 2026-11-01 with templates/test/run_test.sh (19 shot stills checked; framing fixed on the X-ray, slow-mo and cooler shots).
