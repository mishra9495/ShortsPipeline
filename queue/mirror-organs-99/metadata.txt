DESCRIPTION
Rose Marie Bentley lived to 99 and never knew her liver and stomach were on the opposite sides of her body. First-year medical students at Oregon Health & Science University found out when they examined her heart in their gross anatomy class after she donated her body to science.

Her condition was situs inversus with levocardia: the liver, stomach and other abdominal organs were mirrored right to left, while the heart stayed on the normal left side. It occurs in about 1 in 22,000 births and often comes with life-threatening heart problems, yet she became the oldest known person with it.

Source: OHSU News, "Body donor's rare anatomy offers valuable lessons" (8 April 2019). Also CNN Health, "Rose Marie Bentley lived for 99 years with organs in all the wrong places and never knew it" (8 April 2019).
