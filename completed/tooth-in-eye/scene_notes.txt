RULES THIS SCENE FOLLOWS
- No on-screen text of any kind (no "text pops", labels, counters or calendars). Narration and colour carry meaning.
- Characters act procedurally on the mixamorig bones via actor.py. No Mixamo FBX is used (see animation_manifest.txt
  in the repo root: the library is an action/combat pack and retarget.py is unvalidated).
- Every camera is a shot.py spline already moving on the cut. shake=0 everywhere (owner's rule). DOF from subject size.
- Timing comes only from the beats JSON. SYNC fractions below are identical in mix.py:
  PILL 0.18 of beat 2 | CLOUD 0.60 of beat 2 | EXTRACT 0.55 of beat 4 | SHAVE 0.15, DRILL 0.40, LENS 0.70 of beat 5
  SEAT 0.70 of beat 7 | FOCUS 0.35 of beat 9 | GLINT 0.60 of beat 10
