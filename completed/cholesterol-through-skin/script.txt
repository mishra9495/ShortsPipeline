This man ate so much cheese, cholesterol started leaking through his skin.
He was eight months into an all-meat carnivore diet.
Up to nine pounds of cheese and butter, every day.
He said he'd never felt better: more energy, less weight.
Then yellow lumps appeared on his palms, soles, and elbows.
Doctors ran his blood work and were stunned.
His cholesterol had shot past one thousand milligrams per deciliter.
That's nearly five times what doctors call healthy.
Those yellow bumps were pure fat, pushed straight out of his blood.
He felt incredible. His arteries were quietly in danger.
