hiccups-68-years: scene direction (v3 toolkit: kit.py + actor.py + shot.py; one Actor, five sets; see scene.py)

Cast: him = blend/char_hero.blend. No second character.
Sets: HOME (sunny living room, eight photo frames on the wall, window right), BARN (1922 wooden barn, hay bales, lantern, a pink hog built in code),
   BRAIN (X-ray brain in a dark void, brain stem with a red vessel that bursts and an amber "off switch" that dies),
   CHEST (X-ray rib cage: lungs, trachea, vocal cords, red diaphragm dome that snaps down), TALLY (a wall of glowing dots that pop one by one).
   Set changes are hard cuts with a whoosh and an exposure spike. No on-screen text.
Frame 1 (thumbnail): tight face, mid-hiccup, home set, eyes wide.

   -> CordsCU (85mm: vocal cords slam shut with a white flash, repeated).
Beat 7 "That's roughly four hundred thirty million..."  TallyFly (camera flies through a wall of dots popping in a wave) -> WearyFace (55mm, tired, hiccup jolts).
Beat 8 "Yet he married twice and raised eight children."  FamilyWide (28mm orbit, eight frames) -> FramesTruck (50mm truck along the frames, him smiling through a hic).

Beat 10 "He died at ninety-seven, ..."  HomeOrbit (35mm orbit) -> Closer (55mm push-in to a calm smile).
20 shots, every one moving; one slow-motion moment (beat 6).
