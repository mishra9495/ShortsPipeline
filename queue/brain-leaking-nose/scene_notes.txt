SCENE NOTES: brain-leaking-nose  (~44 s total, 10 beats)

ANIMATION LIBRARY NOTE
The repo still has no animation/ folder (`ls animation/` fails: No such file or directory), so there are no
Mixamo FBX filenames to list from it. The only retargeted Mixamo clips the pipeline references live in
D:\ShortsPipeline\blend\anims\ (see scripts/apply_retargeted_action.py):
  - Walking.blend     (action "Walking")
  - FallingIdle.blend (action "FallingIdle")
Every beat below uses one of these two, or the base rig held in a hand-posed static pose ("HOLD").
Where a better Mixamo clip would help, it is marked "PREFERRED IF AVAILABLE" with the Mixamo clip name to
download, run through scripts/retarget.py, and drop into blend/anims/. Do not reference anything else.

Character: the usual female hero variant of the rig (or the base rig with a longer hair card from build_hair.py).
She is a generic character, not a likeness of the real patient.

BEAT 1: "Her brain was leaking out of her nose, and doctors blamed allergies."  (~5 s)
- Camera: extreme close-up on the nose and upper lip, a clear drip forming; snap pull-back to a mid-shot of her
  holding a tissue; quick cut to a doctor shrugging.
- Character animation: HOLD rest pose, keyframed head tilt down toward the tissue (mixamorig:Head/Neck). Doctor
  character: HOLD with a shoulder shrug keyed on mixamorig:LeftShoulder/RightShoulder.
- Key visual: semi-transparent x-ray overlay flashes on: brain in blue, a glowing blue drip line running from the
  skull base down the nasal cavity and out. Text pop "ALLERGIES?" with a red strike-through.
- Lighting mood: cold, clinical white with a blue rim. Dark background.

BEAT 2: "In 2013, Kendra's face hit a dashboard in a crash."  (~4 s)
- Camera: side-on mid-shot inside a car, slow-motion. Then a hard cut to a front POV of the dashboard rushing in.
- Character animation: FallingIdle, trimmed to the first ~1 s, played at 25% speed for the forward lurch into the
  dashboard, then a sharp snap back. PREFERRED IF AVAILABLE: Mixamo "Hit Reaction" or "Head Hit".
- Key visual: motion-blur streaks, a white impact flash on contact, text pop "2013". A quick x-ray flash of the
  skull on impact with a tiny crack line appearing above the nasal cavity.
- Lighting mood: warm interior light that strobes white on impact.

BEAT 3: "Her nose then ran nonstop for years."  (~3.5 s)
- Camera: wide shot, time-lapse. A day/night light sweep across the room. Tissues pile up on the table.
- Character animation: HOLD seated-style pose on the couch (hand-posed hips/knees), with a looping hand-to-nose
  wipe keyed on mixamorig:RightArm/RightForeArm. PREFERRED IF AVAILABLE: Mixamo "Sitting Idle".
- Key visual: a calendar on the wall flipping pages fast, the tissue pile growing, a faint drip loop at the nose.
  Text pop "YEARS…".
- Lighting mood: warm home light, slightly dim, a bit sad.

BEAT 4: "Then a specialist tested the fluid. It wasn't mucus."  (~4 s)
- Camera: close-up on a small sample vial being held up to the light, rack focus from the vial to the
  specialist's widening eyes.
- Character animation: specialist character HOLD, arm raised with the vial (keyed on mixamorig:RightArm). Slow
  head turn toward camera on "wasn't mucus".
- Key visual: the fluid in the vial is crystal clear, not cloudy. Test strip turns a bright colour. Text pop
  "NOT MUCUS" with a short zoom punch.
- Lighting mood: cold, clinical, a single strong key light from above.

BEAT 5: "It was the liquid your brain floats in."  (~3.5 s)
- Camera: internal cutaway. Slow orbit around a translucent head, pushing in to the brain.
- Character animation: none (internal cutaway, the head is static).
- Key visual: the brain suspended in glowing pale-blue CSF inside the skull, gently bobbing. Label "CSF".
  Small arrows show the fluid flowing around the brain and down the spine.
- Lighting mood: cool, bioluminescent blue glow, dark surround.

BEAT 6: "The crash had cracked a tiny hole in her skull."  (~4 s)
- Camera: continue the internal view, fast dolly down to the base of the skull just above the nasal cavity.
  Snap-zoom into the hole.
- Character animation: none (internal cutaway).
- Key visual: a crack and a small hole in the bone above the nose (shown oversized, see fact check), blue CSF
  threading through it and dripping down the nasal passage. A red circle highlight and text pop "HOLE".
- Lighting mood: dramatic, high contrast, a red accent light on the hole.

BEAT 7: "She lost half a pint of brain fluid a day."  (~4 s)
- Camera: split: left half is a mid-shot of her face with the x-ray drip overlay; right half is a measuring glass
  filling with clear fluid up to a "half pint" line.
- Character animation: HOLD, slow head tilt forward (the drip gets worse when she leans forward).
- Key visual: the glass fills in a fast pour, the line "1/2 PINT / DAY" lights up. A counter of days ticks in the
  corner.
- Lighting mood: cold white, clinical.

BEAT 8: "That hole was an open door for bacteria into her brain."  (~4.5 s)
- Camera: internal view again, reversed: start in the nasal cavity looking UP at the hole, then follow a swarm
  through it toward the brain.
- Character animation: none (internal cutaway).
- Key visual: stylized green cartoon bacteria blobs drifting up the nasal passage, squeezing through the hole
  toward the brain. The brain lining flashes a warning red. Text pop "MENINGITIS RISK".
- Lighting mood: dark, menacing, green and red accents.

BEAT 9: "Surgeons went up her nostril and plugged it with her own fat."  (~4.5 s)
- Camera: operating-room mid-shot (surgeon leaning in), then an internal POV travelling up the nostril with the
  endoscope to the hole.
- Character animation: surgeon uses Walking (2 steps to the table, then stop and HOLD leaning in). Patient HOLD
  lying pose (hand-posed, rig rotated flat). PREFERRED IF AVAILABLE: Mixamo "Lying Down" / "Sleeping Idle".
- Key visual: a thin endoscope tube rises through the nasal cavity, a small yellow fat graft is pressed into the
  hole and the blue drip stops. A satisfying "sealed" check mark. Text pop "NO BRAIN SURGERY".
- Lighting mood: bright surgical white with a cool blue monitor glow.

BEAT 10: "If your runny nose is one-sided and salty, get it checked."  (~5 s)
- Camera: close-up face to camera, then pull back to a mid-shot as she walks toward camera, no tissue in hand.
- Character animation: Walking toward camera, 3-4 steps, then stop and HOLD with a small confident nod.
- Key visual: a quick 3-item checklist slides in on the right: "ONE NOSTRIL", "WORSE BENDING FORWARD",
  "SALTY TASTE". End card: "🧠 GET IT CHECKED".
- Lighting mood: bright, warm daylight, hopeful.
