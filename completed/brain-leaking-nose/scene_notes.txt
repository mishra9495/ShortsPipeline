SCENE NOTES: brain-leaking-nose v2 (60 fps, about 28-33 s, 17 shots, no captions or on-screen text)

Everything is modelled in Blender by scene.py (scripts/kit.py): living room, clinic, night road with two cars,
operating room, and X-ray anatomy with a folded brain. Characters are MPFB humans: HER (female lead, built from
scripts/preset_heroine.json) and the DOCTOR (char_hero in a white coat, with a stethoscope, plus a cap and mask in the OR).

BEAT 1 "Her brain was leaking out of her nose, and doctors blamed allergies."
  a. Nose ECU at home: a glossy droplet swells and falls; slow push-in.
  b. X-ray side view: glowing brain fluid runs from the skull base down the nose.
  c. Clinic, over the doctor's shoulder: he shrugs and offers tissues; she sniffs and wipes.
BEAT 2 "In 2013, Kendra's face hit a dashboard in a crash."
  a. Night road, low wide shot behind her red car: headlights rush in.
  b. Impact: the silver car rear-ends hers, with a camera shake and a flash.
  c. Through the passenger window, slow motion: her face lurches into the dashboard.
  d. X-ray flash: a crack opens in the skull base.
BEAT 3 "Her nose then ran nonstop for years."  Living-room time-lapse: day/night cycles through the window, a
  growing tissue pile, she reaches for the box and wipes again and again.
BEAT 4 "Then a specialist tested the fluid. It wasn't mucus."  Clinic: the doctor raises a vial of clear fluid
  to the light; macro of the test strip turning bright green; his eyebrows jump.
BEAT 5 "It was the liquid your brain floats in."  X-ray orbit: the brain floating in pulsing blue fluid.
BEAT 6 "The crash had cracked a tiny hole in her skull."  X-ray push-in to the skull-base hole; cracks glow red.
BEAT 7 "She lost half a pint of brain fluid a day."  Living room: she leans forward, the drip speeds up, and the
  measuring jug in the foreground fills.
BEAT 8 "That hole was an open door for bacteria into her brain."  X-ray: green bacteria crawl up the nose and
  through the hole; the brain lining flashes red.
BEAT 9 "Surgeons went up her nostril and plugged it with her own fat."  OR: she lies under the surgical lamp,
  and the masked surgeon works at her nose. Then X-ray: the endoscope climbs the nostril and a yellow fat graft
  seals the hole (the ring turns green).
BEAT 10 "If your runny nose is one-sided and salty, get it checked."  Home in daylight: she is healthy, smiles,
  and nods to camera while the camera pulls back.

Acting is procedural on the mixamorig bones (scripts/actor.py): breathing, idle sway, blinks, sniffs, reaching
for the tissue box, wiping, the crash whip and lurch, a dazed head roll, the doctor's shrug, a raised vial and a
surprised look, a sedated pose on the OR table, and a final smile and nod. All motion is eased (smootherstep)
and baked per frame at 60 fps.
